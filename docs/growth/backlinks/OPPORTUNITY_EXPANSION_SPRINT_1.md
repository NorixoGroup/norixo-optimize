# Opportunity Expansion — Sprint 1

Status: Draft  
Owner: Norixo  
Program: Growth

## Scope and selection

This sprint reviews the first twenty eligible domains in the normalized global discovery sequence. The pilot domains were excluded. Because `hoteltechreport.com` is already covered by pilot discovery despite appearing in the historical global-discovery table, the sprint includes the next eligible domain, `visitbritain.org`, to retain a twenty-domain lot.

The review is page-level only. It records an opportunity only when a real public page was observed with a direct editorial connection to a currently approved Norixo asset. `Qualified` indicates sufficient observable evidence for a later page and policy review; `Needs Review` indicates a relevant context but an unverified editorial-access, currency, or metric-fit constraint. No contact research or outreach was performed.
