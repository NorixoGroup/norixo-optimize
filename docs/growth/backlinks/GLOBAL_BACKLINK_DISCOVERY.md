# Global Backlink Discovery

Status: Discovery draft  
Owner: Norixo  
Program: Growth  
Scope: North America, Europe, Africa and Middle East  
Last reviewed: 2026-07-27

## Purpose

This document records newly discovered, editorially relevant domains for a future international backlink portfolio. It is a discovery record, not a campaign plan, CRM, contact list, or outreach queue.

Each entry was manually checked for an observable connection to hospitality, short-term rentals, revenue management, travel technology, tourism data, accommodation, or related research. Inclusion does not imply that the publisher accepts external links, endorses Norixo, or should be contacted without a separate qualification review.

## Exclusions and operating rules

- The 10 domains in the pilot portfolio remain unchanged and are excluded from this document.
- The 18 domains in Global Discovery V1.1 are also excluded to prevent duplication.
- No contact, author, email address, outreach message, campaign status, or CRM data is recorded here.
- Every future use must comply with `BACKLINK_QUALIFICATION_STANDARD.md` and `BACKLINK_ASSET_MAPPING_STANDARD.md`.
- The recommended asset is an asset family only; no Norixo URL is prescribed at this stage.

## Official normalization taxonomy

The `Editorial category` value for every domain entry uses one of the following canonical values only: `Government`, `Association`, `Media`, `Travel Tech`, `University`, `Research`, `Analytics`, `Revenue Management`, `Software`, `Community`, or `Conference`.

`Primary language` uses the controlled language labels in this document. `Region` uses only North America, Europe, Africa, Middle East, Asia-Pacific, or Latin America. `Editorial compatibility` uses only Low, Medium, Strong, or Very Strong. `Estimated difficulty` uses only Easy, Medium, or High.

Historical phase balance tables are retained as snapshots of their original discovery phases; the domain rows are the canonical normalized source of truth.

## Remaining weaknesses

- English-language sources are still dominant (31/50); this is expected for North America and the United Kingdom but requires later balancing through French, Spanish, German, Italian, Portuguese and Dutch discovery.
- Europe is stronger in institutions, associations and trade media than in independently operated podcasts and newsletters.

- Each proposed domain still requires page-level validation, publication-guideline review and a specific editorial-fit assessment before it enters a portfolio or any outreach workflow.

### Remaining weaknesses

- The Africa and Middle East set is intentionally weighted toward public tourism and statistical bodies; these provide credibility but have a lower and slower likelihood of accepting a third-party editorial reference.
- The 35-domain set does **not** satisfy the approximately 15% category-concentration target when broad categories are used: destination marketing and public institutions dominate. It is therefore a discovery pool, not a balanced outreach portfolio.
- Arabic-language editorial media, regional SaaS publishers and university-led hospitality research remain under-represented.
- Several countries are represented by a single destination or public body only; later discovery should add independent media, professional communities and research publishers before any outreach expansion.
- Every entry still requires page-level relevance validation and publication-guideline review before portfolio inclusion.
