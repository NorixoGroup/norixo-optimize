# NORIXO — LINK AUTHORITY CAMPAIGN

Status:
Draft

Owner:
Norixo

Program:
Growth

# Purpose

Build Norixo's editorial authority progressively through natural, high-quality backlinks. The campaign prioritizes quality over quantity and focuses on links that are relevant, editorially justified, and durable.

# Success metrics

- Referring domains
- Editorial backlinks
- Referral traffic
- Google Search Console impressions
- Organic clicks
- Organic conversions
- Mentions
- Reply rate
- Conversation rate

# Scope

This campaign covers only the following Norixo assets:

- ADR Calculator
- Occupancy Calculator
- RevPAR Calculator
- Research Hub
- Research Methodology
- Free Market Snapshot

No other asset is in scope for this campaign.

# Campaign phases

## Phase 1 — Preparation

Validate the approved assets, target portfolio, outreach material, tracking process, and campaign safeguards.

## Phase 2 — Pilot (10 domains)

Contact the first ten approved domains, record outcomes, and establish an initial evidence base for the campaign.

## Phase 3 — Optimization

Analyze pilot responses, refine the message and qualification criteria, and prepare the scaled operating approach.

## Phase 4 — Scale (50 domains)

Run the approved outreach process across the complete target portfolio while maintaining the campaign safeguards and tracking requirements.

# Editorial principles

- Never buy backlinks.
- Never use PBNs.
- Never use automated outreach.
- Personalize every message.
- Provide value first.
- Prefer editorial citations.
- Respect publisher guidelines.

# Risks

- Low reply rate
- Poor personalization
- Low-quality outreach
- Inconsistent follow-up
- Irrelevant prospects

# Exit criteria

The pilot campaign is considered validated when:

- The ten domains have been contacted.
- Responses have been analyzed.
- Messages have been adjusted.
- A deployment strategy is ready.
