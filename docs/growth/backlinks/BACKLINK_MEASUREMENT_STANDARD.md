# NORIXO — BACKLINK MEASUREMENT STANDARD

Status:
Draft

Owner:
Norixo

Program:
Growth

# Purpose

This standard defines how backlink campaign performance is measured. Consistent measurement is necessary to improve campaigns, compare outcomes over time, preserve coherent indicators, and support decisions based on documented data.

# Scope

This standard applies to:

- The backlink pilot
- The complete backlink portfolio
- Future backlink campaigns
- Future automation

It defines measurement rules only and does not contain campaign data, results, or implementation details.

# KPI Definitions

- **Qualified Targets:** Portfolio targets that have passed the approved qualification process.
- **Contacts Researched:** Targets for which contact research has been initiated and recorded.
- **Contacts Verified:** Contact references whose information and relevance have been verified before outreach.
- **Outreach Sent:** Initial outreach interactions that have been sent and recorded.
- **Follow-up 1:** First recorded follow-up sent after an initial outreach interaction.
- **Follow-up 2:** Second recorded follow-up sent after an initial outreach interaction.
- **Follow-up 3:** Final permitted recorded follow-up sent after an initial outreach interaction.
- **Replies:** Recorded responses received from a target or verified contact reference.
- **Positive Replies:** Replies that indicate interest, openness, or a constructive next step.
- **Negative Replies:** Replies that explicitly decline, reject, or request no further contact.
- **No Response:** Targets that reach the applicable stop rule without a recorded reply.
- **Links Acquired:** Backlinks recorded as obtained and awaiting or having received verification.
- **Active Links:** Acquired backlinks whose current availability has been verified.
- **Lost Links:** Previously active backlinks that are recorded as removed or no longer reachable.
- **Referral Traffic:** Traffic attributed to a verified acquired backlink when such attribution is available and documented.
- **Campaign Completion:** The recorded state in which all planned campaign actions have been completed, closed, or otherwise resolved.

# Measurement Principles

- Maintain one approved definition for each KPI.
- Do not count the same event more than once.
- Record one event as one measurement.
- Preserve historical records.
- Maintain consistency between the portfolio and the operations register.

# Attribution Rules

Every recorded result must be attributable to:

- A Target ID in the `BK-XXXX` format
- A Campaign ID in the `BL-CAM-YYYY-NNN` format
- An Interaction ID in the `BL-INT-YYYY-NNNN` format when an interaction is involved

Attribution records must preserve the relationship between the qualified portfolio target, the campaign context, and the operational event without changing their identifiers.

# Reporting Frequency

Measurement may be reviewed at the following frequencies:

- Per campaign
- Weekly
- Monthly
- Quarterly

The appropriate frequency depends on the campaign context and is not prescribed by this standard.

# Data Quality Rules

- Use verified data when available.
- Preserve the historical record of measurements and interactions.
- Remove duplicate records through documented correction rather than silent deletion.
- Document corrections.
- Clearly identify any estimate that cannot be verified.

# Success Interpretation

Interpret results by comparing comparable campaigns, analyzing trends rather than isolated values, prioritizing backlink quality over quantity, and considering the relevant editorial context.

This standard does not define numerical targets, benchmarks, or success thresholds.

# Governance

- Document every KPI definition change.
- Preserve compatibility with prior reports when definitions evolve.
- Keep KPI definitions stable unless a documented change is necessary.
- Record the rationale and effective context of any change.

# Future Extensions

No future extension is implemented by this document.
