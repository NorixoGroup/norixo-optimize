# NORIXO — BACKLINK ASSET MAPPING STANDARD

Status:
Draft

Owner:
Norixo

Program:
Growth

# Purpose

This standard defines how Norixo asset families are associated with target domain families and editorial angle families. It provides a consistent governance framework for determining whether an asset is suitable for promotion and how its editorial value should be represented.

# Scope

This standard applies to the selection and governance of Norixo assets considered for backlink activity. It is independent of any individual campaign, target, prospect, contact process, or outreach message.

# Norixo Asset Families

Norixo assets may be classified by their primary editorial function:

- Educational resources
- Methodology and reference documentation
- Interactive tools
- Research-oriented resources
- Market-oriented resources
- Product discovery resources

An asset family describes the role an asset serves for an audience. It does not determine promotion eligibility by itself.

# Target Domain Families

Target domains may be classified by the audience and editorial context they serve:

- Specialized publications
- Educational publishers
- Professional organizations
- Complementary software publishers
- Research-oriented publishers
- Community publishers
- Audio or newsletter publishers

Domain-family classification is used to evaluate relevance and fit. It does not constitute domain approval.

# Editorial Angle Families

Editorial angles may be classified by the value they provide to an audience:

- Definition and explanation
- Practical application
- Methodology and transparency
- Research and evidence
- Decision support
- Professional reference
- Resource discovery

An angle must be selected because it serves the target audience and editorial context, not because it maximizes link acquisition.

# Asset Mapping Principles

Asset mapping connects an asset family, a target domain family, and an editorial angle family. A mapping is appropriate only when the asset provides a clear, accurate, and useful contribution to the target audience.

Mappings must preserve editorial independence. The intended reference must be natural, proportionate to the source content, and justified by the value of the asset itself.

No asset is mapped solely because it is available. Each mapping requires a documented rationale based on relevance, editorial quality, and audience usefulness.

# Link Intent Categories

Link intent describes the editorial purpose of a potential reference:

- Supporting explanation
- Further learning
- Methodology reference
- Research reference
- Practical resource
- Decision-support resource

The selected intent must match both the asset's role and the target publisher's content. It must not misrepresent the asset or imply an endorsement that does not exist.

# Diversification Principles

Asset selection must be diversified across appropriate asset families, target domain families, and editorial angle families. Diversification prevents repeated use of the same type of asset or angle when it no longer represents the strongest editorial fit.

Diversification must not reduce quality. Relevance, accuracy, and editorial value take precedence over distribution across categories.

# Asset Lifecycle

An asset becomes eligible for promotion only after review confirms editorial value, audience relevance, accuracy, and sufficient currency. Publication or availability alone does not make an asset eligible.

Before promotion, an asset must offer a clear editorial reason to be referenced. It must be complete for its intended purpose, stable enough to recommend, and consistent with the governance principles of the active portfolio.

Not all assets are intended to be promoted. Only assets that provide genuine editorial value are eligible. Quality, relevance, and currency always take priority over the quantity of assets promoted.

An asset must be removed from active promotion when it is no longer accurate, relevant, current, stable, or suitable for the intended audience and editorial angle. Removal does not erase the history of prior use.

When a newer asset supersedes an older asset, the transition must be deliberate. The replacement must be reviewed before promotion, the relationship between the prior and replacement assets must be retained, and active references must be updated progressively to preserve portfolio coherence.

Material asset updates, including substantive content changes, improvements, or redesigns, require renewed review of editorial value, relevance, accuracy, and promotion suitability. An update does not automatically preserve active status.

The history of assets used in campaigns must be retained to provide traceability for approval, promotion, update, replacement, removal, and re-evaluation decisions.

# Governance

- Maintain one approved mapping record for each reviewed association.
- Preserve the rationale for approval, rejection, replacement, and removal decisions.
- Do not duplicate equivalent mappings without a distinct editorial rationale.
- Re-evaluate mappings when an asset changes materially or its relevance changes.
- Keep campaign-specific decisions separate from this mapping standard.

# Future Extensions

This standard may later be extended with structured mapping records, review workflows, history tracking, and performance measurement. These extensions are not defined or implemented by this document.
