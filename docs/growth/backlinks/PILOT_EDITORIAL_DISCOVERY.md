# Pilot Editorial Discovery

Status: Draft  
Owner: Norixo  
Program: Growth

## Purpose

This document records page-level editorial opportunities observed on the ten pilot domains. It is a discovery record only: it does not identify contacts, authorise outreach, or assert that a publisher will add a link. Each entry is supported by an observed public page and must still follow the qualification and later operational controls.

## Scope and method

The review was limited to the ten pilot domains specified for Phase 5.3. An opportunity was retained only where a real, public editorial or educational page directly addresses revenue-management KPIs, their calculation, their interpretation, or their methodology. The recommended asset is a factual fit for the reader's need, not a request for insertion.

`Qualified` means the page-level evidence supports a relevant, durable and commercially neutral opportunity. `Needs Review` means the page is relevant but its editorial accessibility, update path, or exact fit requires a later manual review. No contact research was performed.
