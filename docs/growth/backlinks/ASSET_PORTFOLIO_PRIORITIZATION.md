# Asset Portfolio Prioritization

Status: Draft  
Owner: Norixo  
Program: Growth

## Purpose and evidence boundary

This document prioritises the existing Norixo asset families for future backlink discovery and playbook work. It uses only the global discovery, two execution waves, portfolio-yield analysis and RevPAR Calculator playbook already available in the repository.

The evidence base comprises 30 reviewed domains and 16 retained opportunities. It identifies observed editorial fit, not likelihood of a backlink, publisher willingness, authority impact or conversion performance.

## Priority groups

### Assets to develop immediately

These assets should receive the next backlink playbook and discovery attention. “Develop” here means develop the asset-centred backlink strategy, not alter the product or public pages.

- **RevPAR Calculator:** it has the strongest direct calculation fit, four retained contexts and a dedicated playbook already in place.
- **KPI Calculators:** the combined set is repeatedly suited to pages explaining the metric relationship rather than a single formula.
- **Research Methodology:** it matches distinct high-credibility methodology and public-data contexts, and should be handled in a selective, evidence-led track.

### Assets to consolidate

- **Occupancy Calculator:** three direct opportunities justify a dedicated playbook, but every future discovery must check available-night and adjusted-occupancy definitions.
- **ADR Calculator:** it should remain part of the KPI cluster and receive a dedicated playbook only after additional standalone evidence is observed. It is not yet supported as a first-choice independent asset.

### Assets to defer to a later phase

- **Free Market Snapshot:** no retained opportunity currently supports it. Retain it in the asset map, but do not make it a priority for discovery or playbook production until observed page-level demand emerges.

## Portfolio guidance

1. Select the asset after confirming the page’s explanatory need; do not use a high-priority asset merely because it is available.
2. Prioritise evergreen formula, glossary, educational and methodology pages over time-bound reports, news articles and generic resource lists.
3. Match calculator assets to practical application needs, and Research Methodology to provenance, data-quality and interpretation needs.
4. Preserve the distinction between calculated KPIs and publishers’ proprietary or adjusted metric conventions.
5. Treat the matrix as a living evidence summary. A change in priority requires new recorded opportunity evidence, not a preference or authority assumption.

## Risks and limits

- The sample is KPI-oriented and small; it may overstate the performance of calculator assets.
- Opportunity discovery does not establish editorial accessibility or link acquisition likelihood.
- High international potential describes compatibility with the existing opportunity mapping, not translated-content performance or market demand.
- Zero observed opportunities for an asset is an absence of evidence in this sample, not a finding that the asset cannot earn links.
