# NORIXO — BACKLINK QUALIFICATION STANDARD

Status:
Draft

Owner:
Norixo

Program:
Growth

# Purpose

This standard defines the criteria used to qualify domains for Norixo's backlink portfolio. It ensures that every domain is assessed consistently, regardless of the size, timing, or campaign phase of the portfolio.

The standard exists to protect editorial quality, preserve a durable approach to authority building, and prevent inconsistent qualification decisions.

# Scope

This standard applies to:

- The backlink pilot.
- The complete backlink portfolio.
- Future backlink campaigns.

It applies before a domain is added to an approved portfolio or contacted for outreach.

# Qualification Principles

- Quality takes priority over quantity.
- Editorial relevance is required.
- Links must be natural and editorially justified.
- Relationships should be approached with a long-term perspective.
- Manipulative link-building practices are not permitted.

# Eligible Domain Types

The following domain types may be considered when they meet the qualification criteria:

- Specialized blogs
- Specialized media
- Complementary SaaS websites
- Professional associations
- Tourism organizations
- Universities
- Research publications
- Podcasts
- Newsletters
- Communities

Eligibility depends on the quality and fit of an individual domain. Category membership alone does not qualify a domain.

# Non Eligible Domains

The following domain types must not be included in the portfolio:

- Link farms
- Private blog networks (PBNs)
- Spam directories
- AI-generated websites without editorial review
- Link-exchange networks
- Undisclosed paid links
- Penalized domains
- Low-quality automated content sites

# Qualification Criteria

A domain may be accepted only when its review supports the following criteria:

- Thematic relevance
- Editorial quality
- Publication consistency
- Credibility
- Transparency
- Reputation
- Alignment with Norixo
- Realistic potential for a natural editorial link

These criteria are assessed qualitatively. This standard does not impose numerical thresholds.

# Editorial Fit

Editorial fit is established by connecting:

Domain

↓

Norixo asset

↓

Editorial angle

The selected Norixo asset must be useful to the domain's audience. The editorial angle must improve, clarify, or complement the target's existing content without requiring an artificial link placement.

# Rejection Reasons

A domain must be rejected when one or more of the following applies:

- Its audience is not relevant to the Norixo asset.
- Its content lacks credible editorial oversight.
- Its publication practices appear manipulative or primarily link-driven.
- A natural editorial reason for a link cannot be identified.
- Its reputation, transparency, or quality cannot be verified sufficiently.
- It conflicts with the non-eligible domain rules.

# Portfolio Governance

- Validate a domain once before it enters the approved portfolio.
- Maintain one source of truth for validated domains.
- Preserve qualification and interaction history.
- Do not create duplicate domain records.
- Allow re-evaluation when material information changes.

# Future Extensions

This standard may later be extended with:

- Qualification scoring
- CRM integration
- Response tracking
- Acquired-backlink tracking
- Performance metrics

These extensions are not defined or implemented by this document.
