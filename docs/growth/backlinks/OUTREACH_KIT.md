# Purpose

Centralize Norixo's methods for approaching editorial partners. This document is the single internal reference for all outreach activity.

# Communication channels

The approved outreach channels are:

- Email
- LinkedIn
- Reddit
- Slack
- Discord

Channel-specific messages and conditions remain to be defined.

# Outreach workflow

1. Qualification
2. Research the appropriate contact
3. Personalization
4. First contact
5. Follow-up 1
6. Follow-up 2
7. Follow-up 3
8. Closure

# Personalization principles

- Always read the target's content.
- Cite a relevant article when possible.
- Adapt the angle to the target.
- Never send a generic message.
- Provide genuine value.

# Follow-up policy

- Send a maximum of three follow-ups.
- Never spam.
- Stop after no response.
- Record every interaction in the CRM.

# Success indicators

- Reply rate
- Positive reply rate
- Conversations
- Editorial backlinks
- Referral traffic
