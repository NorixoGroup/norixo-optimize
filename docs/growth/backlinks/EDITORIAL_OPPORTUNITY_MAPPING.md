# Editorial Opportunity Mapping

Status: Draft  
Owner: Norixo  
Program: Growth

## Purpose

This document defines the editorial opportunity taxonomy used to assess qualified backlink domains. It does not create outreach campaigns, identify contacts, or prescribe target pages. `GLOBAL_BACKLINK_DISCOVERY.md` remains the source of truth for domains.

## Operating principles

- Prefer earned, editorially justified references over placements.
- Match an opportunity to an asset only where it improves the publisher’s content or audience understanding.
- Treat public bodies, research institutions and associations as credibility opportunities, not guest-post inventory.
- Do not infer that an opportunity is available merely because a domain belongs to a compatible category.

## Opportunity priority

### Tier A — durable editorial authority

Research Citation, Methodology Citation, Statistics Citation, Academic Citation, Industry Report, Research Collaboration, Association Resource, and Documentation Reference.

These have the strongest credibility and persistence. They are generally difficult, low-volume opportunities and should never be treated as scalable placements.

### Tier B — practical educational references

Calculator Reference, Guide Citation, Tutorial Reference, Educational Resource, Training Resource, Community Resource, and Newsletter Mention.

These are useful when content already has a specific explanatory gap. They can be repeatable but must remain audience-first and editorially justified.

### Tier C — conditional or low-persistence opportunities

Tool Directory, Recommended Tools, Roundup Article, Forum Resource, Comparison Guide, Interview, Podcast Guest, Expert Quote, Expert Commentary, Press Mention, and Conference Speaker.

These can be valuable in the right context but vary significantly in editorial control, durability and qualification requirements.

## Editorial playbooks

### Playbook: Government

Propose a methodology, statistics or research-context reference only where an official dataset, tourism report or public measurement discussion already exists. Promote Research Methodology. Seek a citation or open-data reference; do not propose guest content or partner links.

### Playbook: Media

Offer a concise metric clarification, neutral expert context, calculator reference or research explanation that improves a current editorial topic. Promote the relevant KPI Calculator first, then Research Methodology where data provenance is central. Seek a contextual editorial citation.

### Playbook: Software and Travel Tech

Propose a documentation reference, tutorial supplement or calculator reference tied to an existing workflow. Promote the specific KPI Calculator or Free Market Snapshot. Seek a documentation, tutorial or curated-resource link rather than a reciprocal relationship.

### Playbook: Association

Propose training, association-resource or industry-report support that improves member education. Promote KPI Calculators for practical learning and Research Methodology for standards or data interpretation. Seek a maintained learning-resource citation.

### Playbook: University and Research

Propose methodology, academic citation or research-collaboration context only when there is genuine evidence relevance. Promote Research Methodology or Research Hub. Seek a reading-list, methodology or publication citation.

### Playbook: Community

Propose one practical resource for a clearly identified recurring knowledge need. Promote a KPI Calculator or Free Market Snapshot. Seek a moderated community-resource, tutorial or newsletter reference; never post unsolicited promotional links.

### Playbook: Conference

Propose a data-literacy session, expert commentary or research contribution that creates a useful event resource. Promote Research Hub or Research Methodology. Seek a speaker, session-resource or industry-report citation.

## Governance

An opportunity must be selected after—not before—page-level editorial-fit review. The opportunity type, asset family, publisher category and intended editorial value must remain consistent with the qualification and asset-mapping standards.
