## Purpose and evidence base

This analysis consolidates only the evidence already recorded in `PILOT_EDITORIAL_DISCOVERY.md` and `OPPORTUNITY_EXPANSION_SPRINT_1.md`. It does not add domains, pages, URLs, contacts or research.

### Category interpretation

The strongest repeatable evidence is from **specialist software and revenue-management publishers**: they contain reader-oriented metric explanations with formulas, examples and operational interpretation. **Associations, public bodies, research repositories and broad communities** generated less immediate page-level yield, even where their authority is high. Their low observed yield must not be interpreted as low strategic value; the sample measures public editorial contexts, not authority or future partnership value.

## Cross-wave patterns

1. **Evergreen educational pages outperform news and report files.** Formula, definition, convention and interpretation pages repeatedly produced viable contexts. Time-bound news and downloadable reports usually did not.
2. **Metric interdependence matters.** The highest-quality contexts discuss ADR, occupancy and RevPAR together, not in isolation. This explains the recurrent fit of KPI Calculators and RevPAR Calculator.
3. **Transparency creates a distinct opportunity class.** Methodology pages from research-oriented publishers and public bodies support Research Methodology without forcing a product-oriented framing.
4. **Authority is not yield.** Associations, academic repositories and institutions can be highly credible while exposing few public pages that are suitable for a natural third-party reference.
5. **Vendor documentation requires restraint.** It can provide a strong conventions context, but proprietary definitions such as RevPAN or adjusted occupancy must never be silently treated as equivalent to Norixo’s KPI conventions.

## Recommendations for subsequent sprint ordering

### Prioritise first

- **Revenue Management** publishers with public resource libraries, calculator explainers and metric-comparison guides.
- **Software** publishers with maintained educational blogs, glossaries or knowledge bases, provided the opportunity can remain commercially neutral.
- **Travel Tech and specialist Media** only where the page is evergreen, educational or methodology-led rather than transient news.
- **Government and public research** pages that disclose methodology, data coverage and limitations; favour Research Methodology rather than a calculator placement.

### Treat later or selectively

- **Associations**: retain for authority and long-term relationships, but prioritise only public educational hubs over membership, event or report-file material.
- **University repositories and research publishers**: process after a specific maintained educational or citation-friendly page is visible.
- **Community and broad travel bodies**: process only when a direct KPI, methodology or host-education context exists.
- **News-led media**: use only for genuinely current factual context; do not treat them as a scalable evergreen opportunity source.

### Operational sequencing implication

For the next discovery sprint, start with specialist revenue-management and software knowledge bases, then methodology-forward public research sources. Defer authority-first categories that repeatedly yield only PDFs, gated material, event pages or transient news.

## Risks observed

- **Sample-size risk:** 30 domains is directional evidence, not a statistically representative portfolio benchmark.
- **Selection bias:** the pilot was deliberately KPI-oriented and therefore increases the apparent yield of calculator and KPI assets.
- **Editorial-access risk:** a discovered page does not prove a publisher accepts changes, citations or external references.
- **Convention risk:** RevPAR, RevPAN, adjusted occupancy and availability treatments are not interchangeable.
- **Durability risk:** news and periodic reports can become stale or disappear; they should not be treated like evergreen guides.
- **Commercial-neutrality risk:** the strongest vendor-site contexts require a factual, reader-first framing and may still be unsuitable after later policy review.

## Conclusion

The first two waves indicate that the portfolio should be ordered by **page format and topical fit**, not domain authority alone. Public metric explainers, revenue-management guides and transparent methodology pages generated the clearest observed yield. The next sprints should therefore concentrate on those page-rich categories while preserving associations, universities and institutional research for selective, evidence-led discovery.
