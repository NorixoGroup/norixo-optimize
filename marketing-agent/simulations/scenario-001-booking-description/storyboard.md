# Storyboard

## Scene 1

- Duration: 0-5s
- Text: "Nice listing. Weak description."
- Visual: clean Booking-style listing card with attractive photo
- Animation: slight zoom in, then pause on text area
- Voice: "Your Booking listing may look good..."

## Scene 2

- Duration: 5-11s
- Text: "Guests still hesitate."
- Visual: generic description highlighted in pale gray
- Animation: cursor-like scan effect, then subtle fade
- Voice: "...and still lose bookings because of the description."

## Scene 3

- Duration: 11-18s
- Text: "Too vague. Not reassuring enough."
- Visual: phrases like "great stay", "ideal location", "beautiful apartment"
  appear as weak examples
- Animation: weak phrases dim out one by one
- Voice: "Many descriptions sound positive, but too generic."

## Scene 4

- Duration: 18-26s
- Text: "Clarity builds trust."
- Visual: stronger rewritten description card appears
- Animation: before/after slide transition
- Voice: "Guests need practical value, reassurance, and clearer reasons to
choose."

## Scene 5

- Duration: 26-36s
- Text: "New: intelligent Booking description optimization"
- Visual: Norixo-style product intelligence frame around the improved copy
- Animation: highlight lines and trust cues
- Voice: "Norixo now helps optimize Booking descriptions more intelligently."

## Scene 6

- Duration: 36-45s
- Text: "Review the signals your description sends."
- Visual: polished end frame with subtle Norixo brand cue
- Animation: clean outro with CTA reveal
- Voice: "Before touching pricing again, review what your description is really
saying."
