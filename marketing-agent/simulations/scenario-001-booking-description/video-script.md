# Reel Script

## Duration

About 45 seconds

## Structure

### Hook

"Your Booking listing may look good... and still lose bookings because of the
description."

### Problem

"A lot of descriptions sound positive, but too generic. Guests still do not
know why the stay feels practical, reassuring, or easy to choose."

### Solution

"That creates friction. Not always enough to notice instantly, but enough to
reduce confidence when travelers compare nearby listings."

### Feature

"Norixo now helps optimize Booking descriptions more intelligently by spotting
weak signals, missing reassurance, and vague wording that lowers clarity."

### CTA

"Before changing your price again, review what your description is really
saying."
