## Elements To Adapt

- idiomatic flow
- sentence rhythm
- CTA phrasing
- social conventions
- hashtag relevance where needed

## Elements To Preserve

- product truth
- core promise
- feature name logic
- safety constraints
- discreet CTA tone

## Translation Rule

Every localized version must remain faithful to the source Master Content.

No market should receive a stronger or riskier marketing promise than the
source version.
