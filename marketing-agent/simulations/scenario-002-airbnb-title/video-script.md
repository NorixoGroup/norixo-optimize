# Reel Script

## Duration

About 45 seconds

## Structure

### Hook

"Your Airbnb title may sound fine... and still be too weak to help your listing
stand out."

### Problem

"A lot of titles are positive, but too vague. Travelers still do not quickly
understand what makes the stay clear, useful or worth opening."

### Solution

"When the title lacks focus, the listing can lose clarity before the photos and
description even have time to help."

### Feature

"Norixo Optimize helps analyze Airbnb titles more intelligently by spotting
generic wording, missing value signals and clearer ways to express the right
benefit."

### CTA

"Before touching your price again, look at what your title really says in the
first two seconds."
