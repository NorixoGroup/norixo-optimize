# Image Prompt

## Main Prompt

Create a premium social media visual for Norixo Optimize about smarter Airbnb
title optimization. Show a clean before-and-after comparison of an Airbnb
listing title. On the left, display a vague and generic title in muted styling.
On the right, display a clearer and more attractive title with better structure
and readability. Use a modern editorial SaaS design language, warm neutral
background, coral accents, deep navy text, soft sand surfaces, crisp
typography, subtle interface cards, no stock-photo look, no fake metrics, no
aggressive marketing badges. Suggest improved clarity, memorability and first
impression through layout and wording only. Make the design adaptable for
Facebook, Instagram and vertical story crops.

## Visual Notes

- focus on clarity over hype
- show wording improvement, not miracle growth
- keep mobile readability high
- reflect a polished Norixo visual identity

## Variants

- Variant 1: square Instagram feed comparison
- Variant 2: vertical story layout with larger title cards
- Variant 3: Facebook landscape version with a short educational caption zone
