# Quality Criteria

## Role

This document defines the common quality dimensions that Norixo AI should use
to review generated content across all future agents.

## Core Criteria

### Exactitude

Content should remain factually correct and should not invent product features,
results, releases, or unsupported claims.

### Coherence

Content should stay aligned with the scenario, the editorial brief, and the
selected topic.

### Utilite

Content should provide useful value to the target audience and avoid empty
promotion.

### Clarte

Content should stay easy to read, easy to follow, and well structured.

### Style

Content should remain professional, modern, expert, accessible, and never too
commercial.

### Confiance

Content should inspire trust through precision, restraint, and consistency.

### Marque

Content should remain compatible with Norixo tone, positioning, and product
credibility.

### Appel a l'action

CTA should stay discreet, coherent, and useful.

### Reseaux sociaux

Each social variation should respect the expected format and audience behavior
of its platform.

### Multilingue

Future translated content should preserve meaning, tone, product vocabulary,
and CTA coherence.

## Future Scoring Model

A future version may assign a score from `0` to `100` across these criteria.

This scoring model is not implemented in the current phase.
